About the festival

Where sound
meets space.

KAUGE (Estonian for "distant") is a three-day festival at the intersection of experimental music, visual art, and collective experience. Each edition gathers artists from across the globe to a single place in Tallinn — to perform, exhibit, and converse.

The festival takes place across multiple venues in Tallinn's Telliskivi Creative City, blending indoor stages with open-air installations and guided listening sessions.
